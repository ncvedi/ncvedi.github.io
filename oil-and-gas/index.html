Oil & Gas

Notes on upstream, midstream, and downstream oil & gas: exploration economics, drilling activity, capital discipline, and how the industry is adapting to a more volatile pricing environment.

  • Upstream economics. Break-even costs, decline curves, and how shale operators have shifted from growth-at-all-costs to free cash flow discipline.
  • Midstream infrastructure. Pipelines, storage, and the permitting bottlenecks that increasingly determine which projects get built.
  • Refining and downstream. Margins, utilization, and the slow reshaping of refining capacity as demand growth flattens in mature markets.
  • LNG. The buildout of export capacity and how it's rewiring global gas markets.
  • Policy and geopolitics. OPEC+ dynamics, sanctions, and how energy security concerns are reshaping investment decisions.
