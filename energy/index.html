Energy

Notes on the broader energy landscape: how power is generated, stored, and delivered, and how the transition toward lower-carbon sources is playing out against the need for reliable, affordable supply.

  • Grid infrastructure. Why transmission capacity, not generation, is increasingly the binding constraint on new power projects.
  • Renewables. Solar and wind cost curves, intermittency, and the storage buildout needed to make them dispatchable.
  • Nuclear. The renewed interest in nuclear power, including small modular reactors, as a firm low-carbon source.
  • Baseload and reliability. How grid operators are managing the trade-off between decarbonization and reliability during the transition.
  • Demand growth. Data centers, electrification, and industrial demand pushing electricity consumption up after two decades of flat growth.
