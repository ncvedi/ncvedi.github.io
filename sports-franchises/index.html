Sports Franchises

Notes on the business of professional sports franchises: why valuations keep rising even as media economics get more uncertain, and the new sources of capital reshaping who owns teams.

  • Franchise valuations. The fixed, rarely-expanding number of major league franchises makes owning one closer to holding a scarce trophy asset than a normal cash-flow business, and recent sale prices — the Washington Commanders at roughly 6.05 billion dollars in 2023, the Denver Broncos at roughly 4.65 billion dollars in 2022 — imply revenue multiples far above what a typical private business would command.
  • Media rights and broadcasting. Live sports has become one of the last forms of reliably-watched-in-real-time television, and rights fees have kept climbing even as linear TV audiences erode broadly elsewhere; the NFL's current combined media rights deals are worth over 100 billion dollars across roughly a decade. Streaming entrants like Amazon, Apple, and Netflix are now bidding for packages specifically as subscriber acquisition tools.
  • Private equity and institutional ownership. Major leagues traditionally banned institutional investors from owning stakes in teams, but the NBA, MLB, and NHL have all opened the door to passive private equity ownership since 2020 as franchise prices outran what pools of individually wealthy owners could easily finance alone.
  • Stadiums and real estate. As publicly financed stadiums become less politically viable, more owners are self-funding new venues or partnering with developers to build stadiums as anchors for larger mixed-use districts, capturing real estate and non-gameday revenue rather than relying on ticket sales alone.
  • Global expansion. U.S. leagues are playing a growing number of regular-season games abroad to build international media rights value, even as the reverse trend — European soccer building an enormous U.S. fan base through preseason tours and broadcast deals — has made the U.S. one of the Premier League's most valuable single markets despite hosting no domestic team.
