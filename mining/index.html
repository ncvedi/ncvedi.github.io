Mining

Notes on mining and critical minerals: the supply chains behind batteries, electronics, and infrastructure, and the permitting and financing hurdles that stand between resources in the ground and usable supply.

  • Critical minerals. Lithium, copper, nickel, and rare earths, and why demand projections keep outrunning planned supply.
  • Permitting. Why new mines in most Western countries take a decade or more to reach production, and what's being proposed to fix it.
  • Processing and refining. The often-overlooked midstream of mining, where a small number of countries dominate capacity.
  • ESG and community impact. Balancing extraction with environmental and local community considerations.
  • Capital markets. How financing cycles for junior miners shape which projects actually get built.
