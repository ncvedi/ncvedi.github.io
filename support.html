Help with Illyria for iPhone and iPad

Questions, bug reports or requests? Email hi@illyria.app.

Restoring Illyria Pro

If you already subscribe, for example on a new device or after reinstalling, open Settings, tap Illyria Pro, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancelling your subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. In Illyria, open Settings, tap Illyria Pro, then Manage Subscription. Or open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Pro stays active until the end of the period you’ve paid for. Your saved lines and notes stay, and you can keep reviewing cards and following plans you’ve already started.

Downloading recordings

Open a play’s page from the library and tap Download under Listen along. Downloads wait for Wi-Fi unless you change that in Settings → Downloaded Audio, where you can also see how much space recordings use and delete them. Once downloaded, recordings play offline.

Moving to a new iPhone or iPad

With Illyria Pro, your reading progress, marks, notes, folders, plans and flashcards sync between your iPhone and iPad through your private iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple Account on both and they appear on their own.

Without Pro, restoring your device from an iCloud or computer backup brings your reader data with it. You can also open Settings and choose Back Up Saved Lines and Notes, then use Restore from a Backup on the new device. That file covers your marks, typed and handwritten notes, folders, colour names and read-through records. Recordings need to be downloaded again.

Deleting saved reader data

Open Reading settings, choose Delete All Saved Reader Data, and confirm. This removes your reading progress, notes, highlights and their colour names, bookmarks, folders, custom motifs, flashcard progress, reading plan progress and Rehearse recordings from the app. An iCloud backup keeps a copy until your device backs up again.

Something else?

Email hi@illyria.app. For a bug, it helps to include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. You can also read the Illyria Privacy Policy.
